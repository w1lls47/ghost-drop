Welcome to Ghost-Drop

A shelf that lives in the corner of your screen. Drop files onto it, drag them into any app. Five quick things — then it stays out of your way for good.

📥

Drop anything on it

Drag a file from Explorer, a browser download bar, or a web image straight onto the icon. It lights up teal while you're hovering, then catches the file. The file is copied to your shelf and stays there across restarts.

📤

Drag it out — it stays

Drag the icon into Premiere, Photoshop, Discord, or any folder. Ghost-Drop hands the file off as if it came from your real drive. The file stays on the shelf after you drag it out — drag it again as many times as you need. Hit the × button to remove it when you're done.

🗂️

Hover to open the shelf

Move your mouse to the icon and it expands to list everything cached. Click to select files, drag the grip ⠿ to reorder, move the mouse away and it folds closed. Drag one row out, drag your selection out, or drag the icon itself for everything.

🗑️

Removing files

Hover the shelf to reveal the × on each row — that removes the item from the shelf list. Ghost-Drop also quietly sweeps copies older than 30 days on the next launch. Right-click the tray icon to check for updates or quit.

✨

That's it

No save dialogs. Files persist at %APPDATA%\Ghost-Drop\Shelf so your Premiere sequences stay linked even after a restart. Quit anytime from the tray — your shelf stays safe.
