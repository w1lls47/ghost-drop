Welcome to Ghost-Drop

A tiny shelf that lives on your screen. Drop files on it, drag them into any app, and it cleans up after itself. Four quick things to know — then it's out of your way for good.

📥

Drop anything on it

Drag a file from a browser download, Explorer, anywhere — onto the faint icon in the corner of your screen. It lights up while you're hovering, then catches the file.

📤

Drag it back out

Click and drag the icon straight into Premiere, Photoshop, Discord, or any folder. Ghost-Drop hands it off like the file came from your real file system — because as far as that app is concerned, it did.

🗂️

Hover to see everything cached

Hold your mouse over the icon and it expands to show every file waiting on the shelf. Click any file to select it, drag the little grip to reorder, and move your mouse away to fold it back down. Drag one file out, drag your whole selection out, or drag the icon itself for everything.

✨

That's it

No save dialogs, nothing to remember to delete. Quit anytime from the tray icon — everything cached gets wiped the moment you do.
